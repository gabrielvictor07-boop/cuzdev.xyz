02 / BEHIND THE BUILD

AI IN THE
WORKSHOP.
PLAY AT THE CENTER.

The tools are new.
The reason is old: make a good game.

AI helps us move from an idea to something playable: code, procedural art, experiments, and tests. It gives a small project more room to try things.

But speed isn't the finish line. We keep what works, fix what doesn't, and judge the result by how it feels to play.

IDEA→BUILD→PLAY→REPEAT

AI-assisted development. Not a promise of generative AI inside the games.
