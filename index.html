02 / Behind the build

AI in the workshop.
Play at the center.

We use new tools to make games we want to play.

AI helps with code, procedural art, experiments and tests. The screenshots here come from the games themselves, and the animated characters use their original sprites.

We still have to check the result: does the combat work, can you read the scene, and is there a reason to keep exploring? We test and revise before keeping a change.

Idea→Build→Play→Repeat

AI-assisted development describes our production workflow. It is not a promise of generative AI running inside the games.
